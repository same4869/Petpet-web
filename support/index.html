Getting started

Adding your first pet

On first launch, Petpet walks you through adding one pet with a photo, species, breed, birthday, and adoption day. You can edit everything later from the Pet Profile tab, and the birthday / adoption day both drive the counters you see on the Timeline and in Insights.

Adding entries

The “+” button on the Timeline opens the entry composer. Pick a kind (note, photo, weight, vaccine, medication, mood, walk), add detail, save. Entries always attach to the currently selected pet.

Switching pets

Free Petpet supports one pet. Petpet Pro supports multiple pets; the pet avatar in the top-right of each tab opens a picker.

iCloud sync

How does it work?

Petpet uses Apple’s standard CloudKit sync through the NSPersistentCloudKitContainer. When you turn it on, your journal entries are mirrored to your own iCloud account — the same account that already syncs your Contacts, Notes, and Photos.

My entries aren’t appearing on my other device

Check, in order:

  1. Both devices are signed into the same Apple ID.
  2. iCloud Drive is on: Settings → [your Apple ID] → iCloud → iCloud Drive.
  3. Petpet is enabled under iCloud Drive: Settings → [your Apple ID] → iCloud → Show All → Petpet toggle on.
  4. Both devices have network. First-sync can take several minutes for a journal with many photos.

If you still don’t see the entries, force-quit Petpet on both devices and relaunch. CloudKit refreshes on launch.

Can I turn off sync but keep the data on this device?

Yes. Settings → [your Apple ID] → iCloud → Show All → Petpet, and toggle it off. The local journal stays intact; new entries simply stop mirroring to iCloud.

Photos

Where are my photos stored?

Inside Petpet’s own Core Data store. Petpet does not add the photos you attach to the system Photos library. Likewise, deleting a photo inside Petpet does not touch the system Photos library.

Can I reuse a photo from the system library?

Yes — the attach action uses Apple’s photo picker. Petpet copies the selected image into its own store, so the two live independently from that point on.

HealthKit & Walks together

Why does Petpet need HealthKit?

Walks together counts your steps and walking distance as time spent with your pet. The feature reads two HealthKit types (step count + walking + running distance) read-only; nothing is ever written to Health, and the data never leaves your device.

I denied HealthKit by mistake

Open the system Health app → your profile icon (top-right) → Apps → Petpet, and re-enable the permissions. Petpet picks it up on the next launch.

Walks together on iPad

iPads don’t collect step data the way an iPhone does. Petpet will show your iPhone’s step data on the iPad only if your iPad has Health data syncing from your iPhone turned on (Health app on the iPad → Browse → Walking).

AI Poetize

What is this and why do I need my own key?

AI Poetize rewrites a note in a more lyrical voice. Petpet intentionally does not operate the AI backend — we want you to control which provider sees your text. The feature uses OpenRouter; the free tier includes several models and the setup is a two-minute task.

How do I set it up?

  1. Create an account at openrouter.ai.
  2. Generate an API key (keys page, not the chat UI).
  3. In Petpet: Settings → AI Poetize → Set OpenRouter API key. Paste the key and save.

The key is stored in the iOS Keychain on the device that added it. Petpet does not sync it to iCloud or to other devices.

It says my key is invalid

Open openrouter.ai/keys, confirm the key is still active, and double-check you didn’t include leading or trailing whitespace when you pasted it.

Purchases & subscriptions

What does Pro unlock?

Multiple pets, the Lifetime Book PDF export, the home-screen widget, the Live Activity for walks, Advanced Insights, and future Pro-tier features.

Why are there three plans?

Because different people buy software differently. The monthly subscription ($3.99/month) is for anyone who wants to try Pro without a long commitment. The annual subscription ($29.99/year) is the mainstream choice and includes a 7-day free trial. The Lifetime purchase ($79.99 once) is for readers who already know the app fits their life and don’t want to think about a subscription again.

How do I restore my purchase after reinstalling?

Settings → Account → Restore purchases. As long as you’re signed into the same Apple ID that made the purchase, Pro will re-activate within a few seconds.

How do I cancel?

iOS Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions → Petpet. Apple controls the cancellation flow; Petpet cannot force-cancel a subscription on your behalf.

The Lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and has nothing to cancel.

Family Sharing

Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase both support Family Sharing. Enable it on the family organizer’s device under iOS Settings → [your Apple ID] → Family Sharing → Subscriptions → Petpet.

Notifications

Vaccine reminders

When you add a vaccine with a “next due” date, Petpet schedules a local notification one week before and another on the due date. Notifications are scheduled on-device; nothing is sent to a push server.

I’m not receiving reminders

  1. iOS Settings → Notifications → Petpet: Allow Notifications on, Banners / Sounds / Badges as you prefer.
  2. Focus / Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing Petpet.
  3. In Petpet: Settings → Notifications, confirm the vaccine reminders switch is on.

Widget & Live Activity

The widget shows stale info

iOS throttles widget reloads. Petpet requests a timeline refresh after every save; iOS usually honors it within a minute or two but may delay it further to preserve battery. Removing and re-adding the widget forces an immediate reload.

Live Activity isn’t appearing on the Lock Screen

  1. iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode: Live Activities on when locked = on.
  2. iOS Settings → Notifications → Petpet → Live Activities = on.
  3. Live Activities require at least 60 seconds of a tracked walk; shorter sessions are treated as accidental.

Troubleshooting

The app crashed on launch

Force-quit and relaunch. If it happens again, try toggling iCloud sync off and back on from iOS Settings. If that doesn’t help, write to 119123059@qq.com with the exact iOS version and the device.

Timeline is slow to open with hundreds of entries

This is a known optimization area. Petpet paginates entries and caches thumbnails; the first open after a cold launch can take a couple of seconds on an older iPhone. Subsequent opens are instant.

I want to delete everything

Uninstall the app from iOS (long-press the icon → Remove App → Delete App). The local Core Data store is removed with the app. If you had iCloud sync on, the records also live in your iCloud account — iCloud Settings → Manage Storage → Petpet → Delete Data wipes them from your iCloud container.
