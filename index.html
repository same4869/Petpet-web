Why we’re building this

Petpet started with a frustrating conversation. A friend who’d had his dog for ten years couldn’t tell me what she weighed at age three. Not because he didn’t care — because he loved her — but because that number was buried somewhere between a text to his vet, a photo in his camera roll, and a receipt he’d long since lost. Ten years. Nine thousand photos. Nothing he could actually hold.

So we’re building the thing he wished he had: a quiet place where every page of a pet’s life lives together — weight, meds, firsts, ordinary mornings. On your iCloud. Not on a feed. Not shared with anyone. Just a book you’ll want to keep.

What’s inside

Petpet is built around a single question: ten years from now, what do you wish you had kept?

Warm daily timeline

Seven kinds of entries — photo, note, weight, vaccine, medication, mood, walk — grouped by day.

Photos, by month

Every photo lives inside its moment. Filter by firsts, outdoor, food, sleep, or vet visits.

Insights that matter

Weight charts, vaccine due-dates, medication streaks. No social metrics, no vanity numbers.

Monthly Recap

A soft one-image summary of the month you just lived together. Share it, or keep it for yourself.

Lifetime Book

Export everything you’ve written as a keepsake PDF yearbook. Print it, email it, keep it on a shelf.

Walks together

Petpet counts your own steps as time spent with your pet. Read-only HealthKit; never transmitted.

Widget & Live Activity

A quiet home-screen companion. A walk timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Private by design

Petpet does not run a backend. Your journal lives on your device. If you turn iCloud sync on, your entries go to your iCloud, not ours.

  • No Petpet-owned server. No account to create.
  • iCloud sync is opt-in and writes to your own Apple ID.
  • HealthKit access is read-only and never leaves the device.
  • No ads, no trackers, no social feed, no followers.
  • Opt-in analytics send bucketed, categorical counts only. No pet names, no photos, no entry content — ever.
