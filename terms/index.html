AI features and “Bring Your Own Key”

Petpet Pro includes optional AI writing features that can rephrase or lightly structure what you have written. These features are powered by third-party language models accessed through OpenRouter. Petpet never sends your content to any model unless you deliberately invoke an AI action.

Where supported, Petpet can use a BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model: you paste your own OpenRouter key into Petpet, which is stored in your device’s secure Keychain and used directly from your device. Your prompts and responses are then subject to OpenRouter’s terms and privacy policy.

AI output is not veterinary advice. It can be wrong, outdated, or misleading. Never rely on it to decide whether to treat, medicate, or withhold care from an animal. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for health decisions.

Health data

Petpet can read step count and walking distance from Apple HealthKit to frame “Walks together” entries. This access is read-only, opt-in, and happens entirely on your device. Petpet never writes to HealthKit, never ships health data off the device, and does not use it to profile you.

Health and activity data from HealthKit are intended as a rough frame for the walk, not a medical record. They are not suitable for clinical use.
